AI Mastery: From Beginner to Expert

A complete, offline, self-paced curriculum for developers who want to understand AI deeply and use it every day, from writing their first prompt to designing agents, MCP servers, evaluation pipelines and AI-native systems. Examples are in Scala and Python.

Why this exists

Most AI material falls into one of two traps. It is either shallow ("10 prompts that will change your life") or it jumps straight into maths and research papers. This curriculum climbs the whole mountain one step at a time. Each level builds on the previous one and ends with hands-on labs and a checkpoint, so you know you are ready to move up.

The goal is not only to "use AI tools". By the end you should be able to:

  • Explain how modern AI models work, why they fail, and how to get reliable results from them.
  • Make AI a real multiplier in your IDE: agents, instructions, skills, MCP servers and review workflows.
  • Build AI features into products: APIs, RAG, agents, tools, evaluations, guardrails, cost control.
  • Use AI to learn any new language, framework or domain much faster, without becoming dependent on it.
  • Make sound decisions about security, privacy, IP and responsible use.
  • Go deep on the internals (transformers, training, inference), read research, and lead AI adoption in a team.

How to use these docs

  1. Go in order within a level. Pages build on each other. Each one starts with learning objectives and ends with key takeaways.
  2. Keep an AI assistant open next to the docs. Every page has "Try it" boxes. Do them, because reading alone will not make you an expert.
  3. Mark pages complete. Use the button at the bottom of each page. Your progress shows in the sidebar and is saved in this browser.
  4. Search everything. Press / or Ctrl+K. Unknown word? Check the Glossary.
  5. Do the labs and the checkpoint before moving up a level.
  6. Plan time. Level 1 takes about 15–25 hours including labs. Spread it over 2–4 weeks and practise daily.
